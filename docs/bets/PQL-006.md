# PQL-006: Position accounting

## Bet and resolution signal

An immutable position derived from ordered Trade records can answer owned shares,
weighted-average cost, market value and unrealized P&L, while preserving the long-only
invariant across first/additional purchases, partial sales and liquidation.

Success is deterministic replay of a synthetic sequence with hand-calculated
answers, explicit rejection of invalid updates, passing regression tests and the
worked learning checkpoint in `notes/003-cost-basis.md`.

## API and accounting policy

`domain/position.hpp` extends `pql_domain`. `Position::empty(Symbol)` creates a flat
position. `with_trade(const Trade&) const -> std::optional<Position>` returns a new
value or rejects without modifying the source. There are no balance setters or
Order-based updates. Only filled Trade records can be applied.

Accessors expose Symbol, Quantity, optional average Price and cumulative realized
Money. `cost_basis()`, `market_value(Price)` and `unrealized_pnl(Price)` return optional
Money so arithmetic failure cannot silently produce a usable financial value.
Empty positions return zero for these amounts and have no average cost.

Buys use weighted-average cost; partial sells preserve the stored average exactly.
Sales accumulate `(fill price - average cost) * quantity sold` as realized P&L.
Liquidation uses exact represented quantity equality, sets quantity to zero and
clears average cost while preserving realized history and replay time. Reopening
adopts the new purchase price.

Remaining basis is derived as quantity times average. Unrealized P&L uses quantity
times the price difference to avoid subtracting two large nearly equal notionals.
Because doubles are approximate, independently computed value-minus-basis can differ
from that result in the last few bits. No financial rounding policy is inferred.

## Invariants and rejected input

- Fill symbol must match exactly and fill times cannot decrease; equal times use
  caller order.
- A sell cannot exceed owned shares, including an arbitrarily small oversell.
- Positive share/value contributions must not disappear through rounding or underflow.
- Non-finite quantities, notionals, combined basis, averages, realized totals or
  valuation amounts are rejected.
- A partial sale retains positive shares and average cost; an exact full sale is flat.
- Failed updates and valuation queries never mutate the position.

The existing primitives guarantee valid positive trade quantities/prices. Checked
position arithmetic adds constraints those individual values cannot express.

## Constraints and unknowns

This is single-currency weighted-average research accounting before fees. It is not
a tax-lot method selection, broker, cash account or persistent ledger. No market data
is fetched, and no strategy or benchmark result is calculated. The upstream ledger
must retain unique fills and supply their replay order; Position stores neither a
transaction vector nor a duplicate-ID index. Marks need caller-controlled timing and
freshness. Costs and corporate actions are not available in the current Trade model.

## Validation

The tests use 10 shares at $100 followed by 10 at $120, then sells of 5 at $130 and
15 at $90. They verify average $110, partial-sale remaining basis $1,650, unrealized
$225 at mark $125, and final realized -$200. Unequal purchase sizes additionally
prove weighting by shares. Other tests cover fractional holdings, reopening, losses,
immutable rejection, deterministic replay, chronology and numerical boundaries.

Executed results and independent role reviews are recorded under
`.codex/reviews/PQL-006/`. Durable replay, costs, cash accounting and market-data
freshness are adjacent future work, not validation claims of this ticket.
